Hands Down · Promethium — full layer map

Canonical moutis HD system (github.com/moutis/HandsDown), rendered for a 3×6+3 Corne. Seven layers total; the six functional ones are shown (QWERTY is just a plain-QWERTY compatibility fallback). Small top-right badge = hold behavior (mod or layer). Layers are reached by holding a thumb/home key on the alpha layer.

How to reach each layer from the alpha layer (all momentary, via layer-tap holds): SYM = hold Space · NAV = hold R (L-thumb) or Enter (R-thumb) · FUN = hold Bksp (L-thumb) or F · NUM = hold K · CFG = hold Mute/Play (outer thumbs), or the CFG combo (both inner-thumb pairs). Home-row mods on the alpha & FUN/NUM layers: pinky=⌃ ring=⌥ middle=⌘ index=⇧ (both hands).
